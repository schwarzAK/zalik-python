The exam can be retaken twice. The first retake takes place by arrangement with the instructor who taught the course, on the days set by the retake schedule. The second attempt takes place before a committee appointed by the department, so it is more formal and stricter. To sign up for the second attempt, write to the instructor at the corporate email, give your surname, group and the name of the subject, and then wait for confirmation of the date. If you missed the first attempt for a valid reason, attach a supporting document to the letter, and then it will be easier to agree on a convenient day. Please note that the points from continuous assessment are kept, and at the exam you can earn up to forty points. For a successful pass the total must be at least sixty points. Check the exact retake dates in the schedule on the department page or clarify them with the secretary during working hours, Monday to Friday, from ten in the morning to five in the afternoon.
